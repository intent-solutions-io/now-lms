# Signing in as a student

Welcome to **Intent Solutions Learn**. This short lesson gets you from "no
account" to "logged in and looking at the dashboard."

## 1. Create your account

Go to **`/user/logon`** (the "Create account" link). You'll enter:

- your **first and last name**,
- your **email address** — this is also your username, and
- a **password**.

Submit the form to create your account.

## 2. Getting activated

New accounts start **inactive** until they're switched on. How that happens
depends on how the site is currently configured:

- **If an administrator activates you** (the current default): an admin turns
  your account on from the admin user list. If you register and can't log in
  yet, this is why — reach out to your instructor or admin so they can activate
  you.
- **If email verification is enabled:** you'll receive a confirmation email;
  clicking the link both verifies your email and activates your account.

If you try to log in before your account is active, you'll see **"Your account
is inactive"** — that's expected; you just need to be activated first.

## 3. Log in

Go to **`/user/login`** and enter your **email (or username)** and **password**.
Once your account is active, you'll land on your **dashboard** (`/home/panel`),
where you'll see the courses you're enrolled in and any certificates you've
earned.

## Trouble logging in?

- **"Your account is inactive"** → you haven't been activated yet (see step 2).
- **"Incorrect login"** → check the email and password. If password recovery is
  available on your site, use **"Forgot password?"** on the login page (see the
  *Resetting your password* lesson). If that link isn't shown, email isn't
  configured yet — ask an admin to reset your password for you.
