# Enrolling and tracking progress

Once you're logged in, here's how to get into a course and work through it.

## Find a course

Browse the catalog at **`/course/explore`**. Open a course to see its
description, level, and sections. The prep courses you're looking for are:

- **Getting Started on Intent Solutions Learn** — you're in it now.
- **Claude Foundations (Associate onramp)**.
- **Building with Claude (Developer)**.
- **Claude Certified Architect — Foundations (CCA-F) prep**.

## Enroll

From a course page, choose **Enroll**. These prep courses are free and
self-paced, so you can start immediately and move at your own pace.

## Work through the sections

Each course is organized into **sections** (one per topic domain). A typical
section has:

- a **lesson** — the reading for that domain, and
- a **practice quiz** — unlimited attempts, with the explanation shown after each
  question so you learn as you go.

Open each resource in order. When you finish a **required** resource, it's marked
complete and your course progress advances.

## Track your progress

Your **dashboard** (`/home/panel`) shows the courses you're enrolled in. Inside a
course, your completion reflects how many required resources you've finished — a
course is complete once you've done all of them.

## Practice quizzes vs. the mock exam

- **Practice quizzes** (in each section) are for learning: unlimited attempts,
  rationale shown.
- The **final mock exam** (the last section of the associate, developer, and
  CCA-F courses) is meant to be taken under exam-like conditions. The CCA-F mock
  mirrors the real exam's shape — ~60 questions weighted across the five domains,
  with a **72% pass mark**.

Work every practice quiz first; save the mock for when you're ready to test
yourself.
